Comprehensive Capabilities

What We Do for Manufacturing Facilities

Beyond simple distribution, we provide full technical lifecycle support from initial frame sizing to on-site preventive maintenance.

Supply & Emergency Sourcing

Stocked warehouse lines of high-demand frames from ABB, Siemens, Kirloskar, and Bharat Bijlee. Fast-track sourcing for specialized, obsolete, or custom non-standard frame builds.

Installation & Commissioning

Field specialists provide on-site foundation inspection, laser coupling alignment, soft foot mitigation, insulation resistance checks, and vibration baseline verification prior to startup.

Annual Maintenance Contracts

Tailored AMC packages for industrial plants covering scheduled thermal imaging of terminal blocks, bearing vibration spectrum analysis, greasing regimes, and emergency replacement spares.

Energy Efficiency Consulting

Plant-wide motor efficiency audits calculating kilowatt-hour savings by replacing aging IE1/standard motors with IE3 or IE4 super-premium drives, delivering sub-14-month ROI paybacks.
